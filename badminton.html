Rules & Format

  • 01

    Best of 3 games — first to win 2 games takes the match.

  • 02

    Each game is played to 21 points. Must win by 2 points.

  • 03

    At 29–29, the next point wins (30–29 cap).

  • 04

    Rally scoring — every rally awards one point.

  • 05

    Fair play is mandatory throughout the tournament.
